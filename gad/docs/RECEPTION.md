\# GAD evidence reception



When Police B submits a transfer, GAD saves it and displays it in

Incoming Transfers as "Awaiting acceptance". Opening the transfer

lets an officer review it. Custody changes only after acceptance.



\## When an officer can accept



GAD checks the sender's signature, the Cardano transfer record,

the fingerprints of attached files, and the previous custody event.

The officer records the physical condition of the item and its seal.



When the checks pass and an authorized officer accepts, GAD saves

the signed receipt, custody event, and accepted status together.



\## Example for testing



At 10:00, Police B submits USB EV-001 under transfer TR-001.

At 10:05, EV-001 appears in the inbox as "Awaiting acceptance".

At 10:15, after verification and inspection, the officer accepts.

EV-001 now appears as held by GAD, with a receipt for TR-001.



If an attachment's fingerprint differs, acceptance stays blocked,

even when the officer records that the physical seal is intact.
